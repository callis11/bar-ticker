FULL SET FROM SCRATCH (4 beds)
====================================================================
  Bed1-Pi-mount.3mf                      1 Pi + fuse plate
  Bed2-Power-supply-plate.3mf            1 supply plate
  Bed3-End-brackets-clips-washers.3mf    2 end brackets (1 with switch notch) + 10 edge clips + 30 washers
  Bed4-Straps-and-legs.3mf               8 straps + 8 legs
Creality Print: open the file, pick the K1C profile. PLA (PETG for the supply plate if you have it),
0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, 30% infill, NO supports.

WHAT HOLDS THE WOOD
  Edge clips (10): 1 per panel on the top edge + 1 on the bottom edge; the 90-degree flange screws into the
     top/bottom 1x4 with 2 x #6 x 5/8" wood screws.
  End brackets (2): one on each outer end; the flange screws into the wood side board (6 x #6 x 5/8").
     The switch version goes on the Panel 5 end.

WHERE EVERYTHING GOES (back of the ticker, panels face down)
  Every seam (4):  2 straps, top + bottom. The middle stays open for the 16-pin plugs.
  Panel 2 middle:  supply plate on 4 legs (supply bolted on first, 2 x M4 x 8 + washers from underneath)
  Panel 3 middle:  Pi + fuse block plate on 4 legs
  Plug every ribbon and power cable in BEFORE screwing the plates down.

SCREWS (panel inserts are M4)
  M4 x 10 + printed washer:  straps 16, edge clips 10, end brackets 4  = 30
  M4 x 30 + printed washer:  8 (plates, through plate + 18 mm leg, ~6 mm into the insert)
  M4 x 8 + M4 washer:        2 (supply to its plate)
  M2.5 x 8:                  4 (Pi)        #8 x 3/4" sheet-metal: 4 (fuse block)
  #6 x 5/8" wood screws: ~35   #8 x 1-1/4" wood screws: 8 (frame corners)
  Wood: 2 x 1x4 x 8 ft -> 2 @ 64.5" (top/bottom) + 2 @ 7.8" (sides)
