BAR TICKER - PRINT LIST (updated 11/25: nothing crosses a seam through the middle anymore)

EASIEST: open the 4 files in "print-beds" in Creality Print, one per bed:
  Bed1-Pi-mount.3mf                     1 x Pi + fuse block plate
  Bed2-Power-supply-plate.3mf           1 x power-supply plate
  Bed3-End-brackets-clips-washers.3mf   2 end brackets (1 with switch notch) + 10 edge clips + 30 washers
  Bed4-Straps-and-legs.3mf              8 seam straps + 8 legs
Settings: PLA (PETG for the supply plate if you have it), 0.20 mm, 4 walls, 30% infill, NO supports.

Single STLs (same parts):
  ticker-seam-strap.stl      8   (2 per seam, top + bottom; the middle stays open for the 16-pin plugs)
  ticker-pi-mount.stl        1   (middle of Panel 3)
  ticker-psu-plate.stl       1   (middle of Panel 2 or 4)
  ticker-leg.stl             8   (4 under each plate)
  ticker-edge-clip.stl      10
  ticker-end-bracket.stl     1
  ticker-end-bracket-switch.stl 1
  ticker-end-washer.stl     30

DELETE (old, they cover the 16-pin plugs): ticker-seam-pi-mount, ticker-seam-psu-base-A/B, ticker-psu-deck-A/B,
ticker-seam-plate, ticker-seam-psu-cradle.

HARDWARE (panel inserts are M4)
  M4 x 10 + printed washer: straps (16), edge clips (10), end brackets (4)
  M4 x 30 + washers: 8 (4 per plate, through plate + leg). Add washers until the screw stops ~1 mm short of
     the insert bottom (check depth with an M4 x 16 first).
  2 x M4 x 8 + 2 regular M4 washers: supply to its plate (bolt it on the bench first)
  4 x M2.5 x 8 (Pi), 4 x #8 x 3/4" (fuse block)
  ~35 x #6 x 5/8" wood screws, 8 x #8 x 1-1/4" wood screws, 2 x 1x4 x 8 ft
  (No M3 needed anymore.)

Edge clips: 1 per panel per long edge. On the 2 panels with a plate, the middle inserts are taken by the
legs, so use an outer long-edge insert there.
