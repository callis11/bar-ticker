How the game buttons work

Sports: tap as many as you want, or All. My teams always shows all four (Lions, Pistons, MSU football, MSU basketball): each one's game if it's on, today or just ended, otherwise its next game with day, date, time and odds once posted. Live games adds games as they start and keeps each one on 15 minutes after it ends. Close games follows games that are late and within one score. Live, Close and Select all stick to the sports you picked. You can also tap any game to add it. With nothing picked, the ticker shows every game in your sports.

LED color boost (richer color, crisper edges)

LEDs wash out soft colors and crush dark ones; this makes photos pop. To put a message on top of a picture, turn on Over the picture on the Message tab.

Show a message

Style

Where it shows

With the ticker (comes around between leagues)
Over the picture (on top of your photo or GIF)

Pick any mix. On its own clears the ticker for just your message. With the ticker keeps games and ParlayLabs going and brings your message around. Over the picture puts it on top of the photo or GIF you sent. The style works everywhere; long messages scroll at the Ticker tab's speed.

Your teams

Favorite teams show in gold, are always in My teams, and get their own alerts. On the real ticker alerts fire by themselves when your team scores a touchdown, gets to the red zone, wins, or gets into a close game.

Save and move your alerts

One-time setup so this page can save to your GitHub (it stays in this browser only):

  1. Open GitHub → new fine-grained token
  2. Name it bar ticker; Expiration: No expiration (or a year)
  3. Repository access: Only select repositories → bar-ticker
  4. Permissions → Repository → Contents: Read and write
  5. Generate token, copy it, paste it here
