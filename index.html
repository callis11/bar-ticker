Show odds

Odds show for games that haven't started, in the scroll and when you hold a game. Switch off for scores only.

Pick games to show

Switch every

Pick one game to hold it on the ticker, or several and it takes turns. Live games follows every game in progress (just one league if you picked a league above), adds new ones as they start, and keeps each one on for 15 minutes after it ends. Close games follows every close game in every league by itself (late and within one score) and scrolls everything else until one gets tight.

Show a picture or GIF

The ticker is 8 times wider than it is tall. Wide pictures and logos look best. GIFs play as animations.

Show a message

Style

Message only clears the ticker for your message (short ones stay still, long ones scroll). Mix into the scores keeps the games scrolling and brings your message around before each league. The style works both ways.

Try the team alerts

On the real ticker these fire by themselves when the Lions, Pistons or MSU score, win, or get into a close game. The TV delay and the alerts switch below work here too.
